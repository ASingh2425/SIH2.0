- **Pixel Provenance:** `chrome.tabs.captureVisibleTab()` data URLs decoded locally into HTML5 Canvas / Image pixel buffers.
- **OCR Provenance:** Recognized text, word bounding boxes, confidence, and `source: 'visual_ocr'` generated by Tesseract WASM engine in `visual_ocr_engine.ts:L140`.
